Manufacturing

At Al Howail, we are a diverse manufacturing enterprise with specialized divisions in plastics, wood, glass, aluminum, and rubber production. Our comprehensive approach allows us to deliver high-quality, customized solutions to meet the unique needs of our clients across various industries. With a commitment to innovation, sustainability, and excellence, Al Howail is your trusted partner in manufacturing.

Al Howail Plastic Factory

At Al Howail, we specialize in the manufacturing of high-quality polyethylene plastics and a range of related products. Our expertise includes the production of HDPE, LDPE, and LLDPE plastics, ensuring versatile applications for various industries. We also manufacture essential agricultural products such as fertilizer, stock feed netted bags, and more. Committed to innovation and sustainability, we deliver reliable and efficient solutions to meet our clients' diverse needs. Partner with Al Howail plastics for excellence in polyethylene and related product manufacturing.

Al Howail Glass Factory

Al Howail Glass Industries is one of Al Howail Group Division, which was found in 2013 by Mr. Mansour Al Subaie - General Manager, after purchasing it from Zamil Glass, one of Zamil Group Division which used to be the most reputable brand in glass fabricator in The GCC and the Kingdom. Al Howail Glass Industries started production in June 2014 and produces all the glass processing mentioned below, while maintaining one of the best quality, production, packing, and delivery service in the glass history. It's market activity is mainly concentrated in Saudi Arabia market, yet it expanding into the GCC and MEA market.
