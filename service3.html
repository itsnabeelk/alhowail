Manufacturing

Kingdomwood products are designed to outlast and outperform traditional softwoods while providing a sustainable alternative to tropical hardwoods. The Kingdomwood process is 100% eco-friendly, giving you the beauty of real wood without compromising the environment. Kingdomwood is leading the way in sustainable and long-lasting real wood. Our award-winning projects have solidified Kingdomwood as the go-to for top architects. Kingdomwood is unmatched in beauty, durability, and sustainability. Kingdomwood. is a set of manufacturing and sales as one of the large modern technologies. The company adheres to the development concept of "Win by quality, integrity management, people-oriented, innovative development". Kingdomwood supplies a wide variety of higher quality of natural veneers, reconstituted veneers and timber at competitive costs for prestigious projects in the Kingdom starting from offices, showrooms, villas, hospitals, buildings, and countless more. Our customers are guaranteed satisfaction as they are assured with the most reliable quality of material and ensured cost-effective timber and veneer products that maintain the high-quality standards and demands of our customers. Continuing to strengthen and grow, Kingdomwood consistently strives for excellence in customer service and product quality. Efficiency & courteous underpins our client service philosophy.
