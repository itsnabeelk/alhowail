About Our Company

About Al Howail

Al Howail is a leading Saudi Arabian conglomerate with a diversified portfolio spanning trading, manufacturing, construction, and real estate. Established in the 1970s by Mr. Mamdouh Aziz Al Howail, our family partnership enterprise has a legacy of delivering notable projects across the Kingdom, significantly contributing to its economic development.

1

Our Vision and Mission

Our Vision Is To Be A Leading Diversified Company With A Strong Regional And Global Presence, Recognized For Excellence In Business Performance And Corporate Social Responsibility. Our Mission Is To Create Sustainable Value By Leveraging Our Diversified Businesses, Strategic Partnerships, And Talented Human Capital To Serve The Needs Of Our Customers And Contribute To The Economic Growth And Development Of The Kingdom Of Saudi Arabia.

2

Our Commitment

At Al Howail, we are committed to:
  • Excellence: Delivering exceptional quality and value in all our products and services.
  • Sustainability: Integrating environmental, social, and governance (ESG) considerations into our business practices to ensure long-term growth and positive impact.
  • Community: Engaging with and supporting the communities in which we operate through philanthropic initiatives and responsible business practices.
  • Innovation: Continuously seeking new and better ways to meet the needs of our customers and contribute to the advancement of Saudi Arabia.
3

Our Values

Our core values of integrity, transparency, and accountability guide our decision-making and actions, ensuring we uphold the highest ethical standards in all our endeavors.
